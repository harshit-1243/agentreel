word and rule, one scarlet plate per frame (off register on display lines, highlighter under the spoken word).
The earlier dark/glow/serif cut was dropped because it read as generic AI output. Every scene shows a real
artefact of run G04-C05-03 (brief card, agent lane with real timings, run_log.jsonl lines, ₹0.00/₹500 meter,
QA checklist, the Editor's own frames). Duration follows the narration (51.0 s) because the user asked for a
≤ 60 s reel and the challenge requires 30–60 s. Voice + music are the pipeline's own mix (Edge-TTS + procedural
CC0 bed), so the reel and the pipeline share one .srt.

their read floor because scenes are paced by the voice; unticked checkboxes are intentionally pale).
