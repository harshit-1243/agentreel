# Brag Plan: AgentReel (G04) — brief C05 reel

## What is this app?
A one-command, seven-agent pipeline built in the LY Agentic AI Lab that turns a sealed brief into a grounded,
captioned 9:16 reel — and logs, costs and QA-checks every step. Its live-run brief (C05) asks for a reel that sells
the *AI-ready graduate* to aspirants and recruiters.

## The angle
**The reel is its own proof.** Every claim in the narration ("multi-agent pipelines", "a ₹500 budget", "every call
logged", "deploy") is shown with the pipeline's *real* artefacts from this run: the brief card, the agent graph with
real per-agent numbers, the actual `run_log.jsonl` lines, the QA checklist, the storyboard frames the agents
rendered. Nothing on screen is a mock dashboard.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Frame 0 already shows the question in big serif type: **"What does an AI-ready graduate ___"** — the last word
lands as the voice says "build?". Thumbnail = the question.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The sealed **brief card C05** flips in; "study AI" gets struck out, **"ship it."** is stamped in crimson.
- The **seven agents** light up one by one with their real outputs (plan.json → qa_report.json); a crimson loop
  arrow snaps from Critic back to Scriptwriter on "critic loop".
- **3:00:00** hard-stop clock + **₹0.00 / ₹500** budget meter (the real spend of this run).
- The terminal streams the run's **actual log records**, then types `python run.py --brief briefs/C05.yaml --seed 42`.
- Four responsible-AI rows tick on the spoken words, each with evidence from the run (facts with page numbers, no
  faces, disclosure card, licence manifest).
- Proof: the storyboard frames the Editor rendered scroll past with the run id and **QA PASS** badge.

## Outro / punchline
"Hire our AI-ready graduates today." lands word by word, "today." in crimson; then the end card: K J Somaiya School
of Engineering wordmark, CTA, **"Created with AI agents — LY Agentic AI Lab"**.

## User flow worth showing
Brief drawn → agents run (graph lights, logs stream, critic loop) → QA pass + finished reel frames.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: confident lab film — "show the receipts"
- Interpretation: fewer, longer holds; type lands then stays dead still; motion comes from the product (rows
  lighting, logs streaming, meters filling), not from decoration. Professional, innovative, authoritative (brief).

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: ≈ 50 s (user asked for a reel of max 1 minute; the brief says 45 s, the Critic allows ±20 %).
The /brag 15–25 s window is overridden by the user's explicit request and the challenge's 30–60 s rule.

## Visual identity (from the project — agents/riso.py)
- Background: #ede9e0 newsprint with fibre noise (no gradients, no glow)
- Accent: #e8412c scarlet plate, 5 px off register, one focus per frame
- Text: #263a8c blue ink; overprint #220f18 where the plates meet
- Display font: Archivo Black, expanded (OFL, bundled via @font-face)
- Body font: Archivo; data/mono: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: the agent lane cards and the terminal run log

## Audio direction
- Role: narrated film — the voice is the clock.
- Music: the pipeline's own procedural bed (Am–F–C–G, 92 BPM, CC0), already ducked under the VO in the
  pipeline's loudness-normalised mix. The bundled ende.app tracks are not used because their licence is unverified
  and the challenge penalises unlicensed music.
- Music treatment: as mixed by the Editor agent (−14 LUFS), fade under the end card.
- Music cue guidance: unavailable for strong-cue locks (narration drives timing); text lands on the spoken word.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — the mix is voice-led; motion comes from the product.
- SFX posture: sparse, CC0 Kenney set — soft key ticks for log lines, a soft impact on the SEALED stamp, drops on the
  four checklist ticks, one bell on the end card.
- Restraint rule: no effect without a cause on screen; never over the voice's stressed words.

## Voiceover script
Generated by the Scriptwriter agent (outputs/script.md, v2 after the Critic's word-budget loop) and voiced by the
Asset Generator (Edge-TTS, en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural). /brag's Kokoro step is replaced by the pipeline's own
voice so the reel, the MoviePy cut and the .srt share one timeline.
